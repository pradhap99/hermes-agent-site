Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how Hermes Agent ("the app"), operated by Pradhap M, handles information from your Google account.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app may access the Google data covered by the permissions you approve, such as Gmail messages, Calendar events, Drive files, Sheets and Docs.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the assistant features you request, for example summarising email, drafting replies, scheduling events or finding files. It is not used for advertising and is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage

OAuth tokens and any data the app processes are stored on the machine where the app runs. The app does not keep a separate copy of your Google data on third-party servers beyond what is needed to answer a request.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except where needed to process a request you make (such as sending content to the language model that powers the assistant) or where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. To have stored data deleted, email the address below.
